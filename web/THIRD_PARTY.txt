The page reads your own Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate file in your browser and sends nothing anywhere; it carries none of the
game's data. Monster Hunter belongs to Capcom; this mod is not affiliated with Capcom or Nintendo.

Programs and libraries the page loads, unmodified, from its pyodide folder:

* Pyodide (Mozilla Public License 2.0), https://pyodide.org, https://github.com/pyodide/pyodide
* Python (Python Software Foundation License), https://www.python.org
* python-zstandard and Zstandard (BSD licence), https://github.com/indygreg/python-zstandard, for Azahar's
  compressed game files
* CFFI (MIT License), https://github.com/python-cffi/cffi, and pycparser (BSD licence),
  https://github.com/eliben/pycparser, which python-zstandard's package depends on
