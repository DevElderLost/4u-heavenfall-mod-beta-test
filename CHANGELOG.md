# Changelog

What Heavenfall Beta gives you. The program's own changes are under "The program".

## Unreleased

### The program
- The web installer reads your game files far less, so phones make the mod much faster.
- While the web installer makes the mod, the screen stays on even after you leave and come back.
- The web installer and the program make the mod from Azahar's compressed games (.zcci, .zcia) again.
- A game file whose name says it is decrypted but is not now says so, and how to decrypt it.

## 0.2.0

### Layered Gear
- Layered Gear at the Smithy: wear the look of any armor, weapon or Felyne gear over your own.
- A Relics list for the Guild Quests' relic gear.
- A free color window for armor looks.
- With Monster Hunter XX's game, its weapon and armor looks too, also from the web installer.
- In multiplayer, players with the mod see each other's Layered Gear.

### Extended Wyporium
- A Rare Materials Exchange for turning common items into a monster's rare material.
- With Monster Hunter XX's game, trades for XX's materials.

### QoL
- Start can skip the camera over a slain monster.
- Start can skip a monster's intro scene and its music when the game allows it, as the touch screen's Skip does.
- The camera can turn up to five times faster.
- Better Camera: the New 3DS C-Stick turns and stops the camera and aim smoothly, at your speed and acceleration.
- With a Hunting Horn, its songs show under the minimap and light up as you play them.
- A horn song whose effect you have turns green, or purple after an encore, blinking at its end.
- Mod Settings can colour an active horn song's name or its background.
- The frame rate can be set from 30 to 60 FPS in fives (emulator only).
- Manage Equipment and Equipment Sets from every item box.
- Damage numbers over monsters, in three styles; switch them on in Mod Settings.
- Mod Settings sorts its options into Display, Camera, Interface, QoL and Other.
- Mod Settings' lists show each page in a frame its size, with no empty rows.

### Real Hardware
- Heavenfall Beta on a real 3DS, installed and kept up to date by the new Heavenfall Launcher.

### The program (PC only)
- The Options page in three modules, each switched on or off whole.
- Monster Hunter XX's file as an option on the Install page.
- A start script for Linux; emulators from Flatpak or unpacked portable are found too.
- HD texture packs keep working with the mod.

### Fixes
- The START menu's Guild Quests list now shows on the top screen (emulator only).
- Turning the lock-on off in Mod Settings now hides the monster's face on the top screen.
- Setting Field of View back to Default during a quest no longer zooms the camera in.
- At very wide screen shapes the target marker is no longer drawn behind the item bar.
- With a smaller HUD, the chat's text in a quest now shrinks together with its background.
- With some HD texture packs, spaces in texts no longer show as dashes.

## 0.1.3

### One screen
- Hunters who chose the other touch layout get the one that keeps the map on top.
- The world map and menus stay in place when the touch bar is at the bottom.
- Registering an equipment set shows the list, skills and pieces, with the yes / no box in sight.
- When creating a Gathering Hall, pinned papers and seals no longer cover the labels and buttons.
- The town map no longer stays in front of the fade to black when something loads.

### Hunting
- Select kicks and only Start opens the menu; with the game's START option at Kick, they swap.
- Holding A repeats picking up and gathering; in quest rewards and the Wyporium, A moves to the next item.
- Holding B speeds through conversation text.
- Mod Settings can turn off the lock-on with L, holding A and fast text.
- The Wystone's timer bar shrinks with the HUD size.

### Cheats
- The cheats can also be changed in the game: START menu, third page, Mod Settings.
- Mod Settings' lists show ten rows a page; Left and Right turn the pages.
- Each character keeps its own choices; one with none yet starts with what the Options page says.
- Cheats can also show weapons' true attack instead of the inflated number.
- Cheats can fit the game and its menus to screens from 4:3 to 32:9; experimental.
- The START menu's Mod Settings can change that screen shape during play, for each character.

### The program
- The mod can be made in a browser on Android or PC, with nothing to install.
- An update program beside HeavenfallBeta brings the program and your installed mod to the latest version.

## 0.1.2

### Hunting
- Talk windows keep their full size in towns and Gathering Halls; only quests shrink them with the HUD.

### The program
- The interface editing tools are in English, and Ctrl+P in the recorder saves a mark to work on.

## 0.1.1 and earlier

### One screen
- The title screen, main menu, quest list, Canteen, yes / no questions and menu descriptions show on the top screen.
- Shops, equipment sets, the Melding Pot, Palico menus, the Gallery, help, tutorials and the Arena show on top too.
- Gathering Hall menus, Guild Cards, friends, chat and gestures show on top too.
- After a quest, the loading screen's paper characters and their message show small on top.
- New characters are made on the top screen and named with the D-pad.
- In towns and Gathering Halls, X opens the World Map on the top screen, with pad controls.
- In towns and halls, a framed town map with the place's name shows by default; L hides it.
- In a Gathering Hall, a box at the top left lists each hunter's weapon, name, Hunter Rank and ticket.
- On the Guild Card (Y) and while editing it (X), a photo mode hides the menus around the hunters.

### Hunting
- L plus D-pad up locks on to a large monster or switches targets, showing its face on top.
- The top-screen HUD and the talk windows can shrink to 90, 80, 70 or 60 percent; it starts at 70.


### Cheats
- 3D is off and glow and distant blur are gone by default, for a sharper picture.
- Cheats can also fix the frame rate at 30 or 60 FPS and widen or narrow the camera's view.
- An online fix can give multiplayer rooms more time to connect.

### The program
- A Windows program, with nothing else to install, puts Heavenfall Beta into Azahar or Citra and can update or remove it.
- You need your own Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate, European or American; the update is not needed.
- It finds your game by itself, as .3ds, decrypted .cia, or Azahar's compressed .zcci and .zcia.
- It asks before replacing any mod it did not install.
- The program is in English and Spanish, and starts in your system's language.
