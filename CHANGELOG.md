# Changelog

What Heavenfall Beta gives you. The program's own changes are under "The program".

## 0.3.0

### Android
- A Heavenfall app for Android runs the game on the phone itself, with no emulator.
- The app makes the mod from your own game, builds the game on the phone and starts it.
- One button updates the app, the mod and only the part of the game that changed.
- The app needs the American game and its 1.3.0 update; Monster Hunter XX adds its looks.

### Layered Gear
- Layered Gear leaves out repeated looks: armor that looks the same on your hunter, and weapons.
- Weapon looks you can only get by upgrading appear in Layered Gear as soon as their rank opens.

### Cheats
- Cheats can multiply the damage your hits deal to monsters, from 1.1x to 10x.
- Cheats can cut the damage your hunter takes, down to 0.05x.
- With other aspect ratios allowed, cheats can fit the game to an 18:9 picture (emulator only).

### Fixes
- On Old 3DS consoles, Gogmazios' quests no longer freeze the game.
- Trying on a Layered Gear look marks only that look, not others sharing its pieces.
- With an HD texture pack, item icons and the quest HUD stay sharp with the web installer too (emulator only).
- Monster Hunter XX's Felyne costumes like Navirou's show their face, not a hollow head.
- Felyne costumes built on their own frame, like Navirou's, move as in Monster Hunter XX.
- The Baki Combat Jutsu look fights bare-handed; its emblem shows only in Layered Gear's preview.
- Monster Hunter XX's shields, sheaths and second blades show no white glow either.

## 0.2.2

### Weapon tree
- Beside your money, the tree shows the price of the weapon under the cursor, grey when you are short.

### Item wheel
- The item wheel starts off in a new character; turn it on in Mod Settings, Radial Menu.
- With the Flick or Release trigger, the wheel uses the item the stick points at when let go.
- The wheel's gold shows the item it will use, and nothing on a push too light to use one.

### Layered Gear
- Layered Gear gains Nargacuga's and Silverwind Nargacuga's looks from Monster Hunter XX.
- Monster Hunter XX's Felyne costumes, like Happy's, show their own head; transforming weapons show their drawn form.
- Every item box has a Layered Gear line to put on the looks you own.
- Your Guild Card shows your Layered Gear, and modded players' cards keep and show theirs.

### QoL
- Your save is MH4U's own, clean; Heavenfall Beta's extras live in files beside it on the SD card.
- Without the mod, MH4U keeps every hunter you saved in Heavenfall Beta; install it again and the extras return.
- A save MH4U would not fully understand is cleaned as soon as the title screen reads it.
- On a 3DS, the launcher's More menu can take your hunter's save to MH4U and put MH4U's save back (3DS only).
- It shows both saves' hunters and play time first, and warns when you would go back in time.
- Both saves are backed up on the SD card before anything changes.
- It marks a slot holding a different hunter in each game, since they would overwrite each other's sets.
- When MH4U is on both the cartridge and the SD card, the launcher asks which one to use.
- Bringing your MH4U save says plainly it replaces all three hunters, and names the backup folder.
- On a 3DS, the launcher's More menu turns the cheats on or off without reinstalling the game.
- The program backs up your saves before it installs or updates the mod.
- Your Housekeeper has a Jukebox: one song or a playlist of the game's music plays in the towns.
- With Monster Hunter XX's game, the Jukebox can also have its monster, area, town and hall themes.
- A playlist can play in order or shuffled, in the halls too, changing song after a few loops.
- The top-screen menus and the smaller HUD take less of the game's time each frame: smoother at 60 FPS (emulator only).
- New characters can skip the sand ship's prologue and start on arriving in Val Habar.
- Upgraded items like Mega Potions show a yellow star, two on level 3 ammo; Mod Settings can make them white.
- Rare materials like gems glow and twinkle on the quest rewards screen; Mod Settings can turn either mark off.
- Honed weapons keep their bonus without the glow; Mod Settings can turn the glow back on.
- Mod Settings can start the town map hidden on arriving; L still shows or hides it.
- Change Appearance works from every item box too, not only at home.
- In Change Appearance, holding L hides the interface so you can see your hunter.
- In single-screen mode, Change Appearance shows no Camera or Back button (emulator only).

### The program
- With an HD texture pack, the weapon tree, item wheel, item stars, damage numbers and target hint look sharp.

### Fixes
- The 3DS launcher no longer says there is no internet when the console's date is wrong.
- The 3DS launcher still finds updates when GitHub limits requests from your network.
- The town map's place name no longer shows over the fade to black and the loading screen.
- While the game loads your hunter, its Layered Gear keeps its colors, not the real armor's.
- Trying on Layered Gear no longer shows your worn look in the new color for a moment.
- Holding A in the Wyporium's Claim Items no longer leaves old icons behind or hides the item's details.
- In Claim Items, the item's icon stays inside its details panel at wide aspect ratios (emulator only).
- At wide aspect ratios, monsters' marks stay on the moved minimap instead of hiding under it (emulator only).

## 0.2.1

### Weapon tree
- Forge Weapons shows each weapon class as a tree of its upgrade lines, with only discovered weapons.
- On the tree, A forges or upgrades right there; a hammer marks weapons forged from scratch.
- Each weapon on the tree shows its element or status, and a box when a copy is stored.
- New weapons on the tree show a pulsing NEW, as in the game's list, until you press A on them.
- A weapon you can upgrade into or craft asks which, showing the materials of the choice under the cursor.
- Upgrades take the weapon you wear first; choosing a weapon in Upgrade Equipment opens its tree to upgrade that one.
- On the tree, X switches between stats, try on and comparison; Mod Settings can switch back to the list.
- Upgrading on the tree plays the smith's hammering and then asks whether to equip the weapon.

### Item wheel
- With a C-Stick, holding L and pushing it uses one of eight items from a wheel, sheathing your weapon first.
- The wheel shows each item's name and quantity; an empty item that can be combined is combined once.
- Mod Settings' Radial Menu edits the wheel's items, place, size, text size and trigger, or turns it off.
- The wheel's trigger can be ZR: point with the C-Stick, press ZR, and ZR does nothing else.

### QoL
- Damage numbers over monsters, in three styles; switch them on in Mod Settings.
- With a Hunting Horn, its songs show under the minimap and light up as you play them.
- A horn song whose effect you have turns green, or purple after an encore, blinking at its end.
- Mod Settings can colour an active horn song's name or its background.
- Start can skip a monster's intro scene and its music when the game allows it, as the touch screen's Skip does.
- Better Camera: the New 3DS C-Stick turns and stops the camera and aim smoothly, at your speed and acceleration.
- The camera can sit from half to twice the game's distance from your hunter.
- Jamo's World-style sharpness gauge: the bar shrinks as each colour wears down; Mod Settings can turn it off.
- The frame rate can be set from 30 to 60 FPS in fives (emulator only).

### Mod Settings
- Mod Settings sorts its options into Interface, Camera, QoL, Display, Effects and Other, most important first.
- Mod Settings' lists show up to ten rows a page, each page in a frame its size; Left and Right turn pages.
- Mod Settings has room for many more options; every character's choices return to their defaults once.
- Field of View, Online Room Fix and True Weapon Attack work on a real 3DS too.

### Layered Gear
- A switch above the Layered Gear rank row shows only the looks you own.
- Mod Settings can hide Layered Gear for each character; everyone then shows real gear, nothing is lost.

### Cheats
- Enable cheats, off at first, adds a Cheats group to Mod Settings; every cheat in it starts off.
- Never play in multiplayer with cheats without telling the other hunters.
- Cheats can make gathering, mining, bug catching, carving and fishing twice as fast.
- Cheats can end the flex after drinking or eating at once, with the stick or a button.
- Cheats can stop sharpening at once by pressing B.
- Cheats can make the end-of-quest countdown run faster while you hold A and B.
- Cheats can stop the timer on the main rewards screens and hide its countdown there.
- Cheats can keep Capture Guru and Autotracker always on for your minimap.
- On a 3DS, the launcher's More menu turns the cheats on or off.

### The program
- The Options page shows only what Mod Settings does not: single screen, Layered Gear, HUD size, aspect ratio and cheats.
- Choices that moved from the Options page to Mod Settings start again from their defaults.
- The web installer reads your game files far less, so phones make the mod much faster.
- While the web installer makes the mod, the screen stays on even after you leave and come back.
- The web installer and the program make the mod from Azahar's compressed games (.zcci, .zcia) again.
- A game file whose name says it is decrypted but is not now says so, and how to decrypt it.

### Fixes
- HD texture packs keep working after the program updates itself.

## 0.2.0

### Layered Gear
- Layered Gear at the Smithy: wear the look of any armor, weapon or Felyne gear over your own.
- A Relics list for the Guild Quests' relic gear.
- A free color window for armor looks.
- With Monster Hunter XX's game, its weapon and armor looks too, also from the web installer.

### Extended Wyporium

- With Monster Hunter XX's game, trades for XX's materials.

### QoL

- Manage Equipment and Equipment Sets from every item box.

### Real Hardware

### The program (PC only)
- The Options page in three modules, each switched on or off whole.
- Monster Hunter XX's file as an option on the Install page.
- A start script for Linux; emulators from Flatpak or unpacked portable are found too.
- HD texture packs keep working with the mod.

### Fixes
- The START menu's Guild Quests list now shows on the top screen (emulator only).
- Turning the lock-on off in Mod Settings now hides the monster's face on the top screen.
- Setting Field of View back to Default during a quest no longer zooms the camera in.
- At very wide screen shapes the target marker is no longer drawn behind the item bar.
- With a smaller HUD, the chat's text in a quest now shrinks together with its background.
- With some HD texture packs, spaces in texts no longer show as dashes.

## 0.1.1 and earlier

### One screen
- The title screen, main menu, quest list, Canteen, yes / no questions and menu descriptions show on the top screen.
- Shops, equipment sets, the Melding Pot, Palico menus, the Gallery, help, tutorials and the Arena show on top too.
- Gathering Hall menus, Guild Cards, friends, chat and gestures show on top too.
- After a quest, the loading screen's paper characters and their message show small on top.
- New characters are made on the top screen and named with the D-pad.
- In towns and Gathering Halls, X opens the World Map on the top screen, with pad controls.
- In towns and halls, a framed town map with the place's name shows by default; L hides it.
- In a Gathering Hall, a box at the top left lists each hunter's weapon, name, Hunter Rank and ticket.
- On the Guild Card (Y) and while editing it (X), a photo mode hides the menus around the hunters.

### Hunting
- L plus D-pad up locks on to a large monster or switches targets, showing its face on top.
- The top-screen HUD and the talk windows can shrink to 90, 80, 70 or 60 percent; it starts at 70.


### Cheats
- 3D is off and glow and distant blur are gone by default, for a sharper picture.
- Cheats can also fix the frame rate at 30 or 60 FPS and widen or narrow the camera's view.
- An online fix can give multiplayer rooms more time to connect.

### The program
- A Windows program, with nothing else to install, puts Heavenfall Beta into Azahar or Citra and can update or remove it.
- You need your own Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate, European or American; the update is not needed.
- It finds your game by itself, as .3ds, decrypted .cia, or Azahar's compressed .zcci and .zcia.
- It asks before replacing any mod it did not install.
- The program is in English and Spanish, and starts in your system's language.
